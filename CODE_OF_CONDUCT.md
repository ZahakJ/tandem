# Code of conduct

Tandem is a small project, made for fun and for people who like playing games on little handhelds. This document is short because the rule is simple.

## The rule

Be someone the next person would be glad to meet here.

That means: talk about the work, not the person doing it; assume whoever wrote the issue or the patch is trying to help; and explain what you think plainly and kindly. Newcomers are welcome, and "I don't know how to do this yet" is a fine thing to say.

Not welcome: harassment, insults, demeaning or discriminatory comments, sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and following someone around the project to keep at them. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else the project is the reason you are talking.

## Piracy

Tandem is a frontend for games you own. Don't post links to ROMs, BIOS files, keys or firmware, or ask where to get them. Such posts are removed, and repeated ones get the account blocked.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or account. If you would rather talk privately first, open an issue that says only that you'd like to report something, and the maintainer will reach out.

## What happens

Reports are read and answered, usually within a week. What follows is proportionate: often a private word, sometimes an edited or removed comment, and for serious or repeated problems a block. The person reported is told what the problem was, and a reporter's identity is never shared without their permission.
